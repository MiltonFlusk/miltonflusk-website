Paper trading only

First rule: no live trades from this site.

Milton Flusk can help you learn market structure and practice a process. It does not place trades, connect to brokers, guarantee returns, or provide personalized investment advice.

Trading involves risk, including the risk of loss. Start with education and paper records before risking real money.

Sample daily rule

If the setup is unclear, the answer is no paper trade.

The goal is not to create action every day. The goal is to learn what clean conditions look like and build the patience to do nothing when the market is noisy.

  • Clear thesis before trigger.
  • Invalidation written before entry.
  • Risk note visible before any paper plan.
