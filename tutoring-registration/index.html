Tutoring Agreement

Private Tutoring Terms and Expectations

This agreement outlines the structure, expectations and responsibilities associated with private tutoring support provided by Milton Flusk.

1. Tutoring Structure

Tutoring sessions are structured around curriculum alignment, conceptual understanding, worked examples and guided practice.

Sessions may include:

  • Homework assistance
  • Exam preparation
  • Revision sessions
  • Baseline assessments
  • Targeted gap intervention

2. Student Responsibilities

Learners are expected to:

  • Attend sessions punctually
  • Bring the required learning materials
  • Complete assigned homework
  • Communicate academic difficulties honestly
  • Participate actively during sessions

3. Parent / Guardian Expectations

Parents, guardians or sponsors are encouraged to:

  • Support consistency and accountability
  • Monitor homework and revision habits
  • Communicate scheduling conflicts in advance
  • Review progress feedback when provided

4. Communication and Feedback

Progress feedback may include comments on:

  • Work ethic
  • Participation
  • Technical understanding
  • Homework completion
  • Areas requiring improvement

5. Scheduling

Session scheduling is arranged according to availability and academic priorities.

Where possible, reasonable notice should be given for cancellations or rescheduling requests.

Agreement Confirmation

Submission of the tutoring registration form constitutes acknowledgement and acceptance of this tutoring agreement and support structure.
