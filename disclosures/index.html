Disclosures

Education first. Paper trading only.

This page explains what Milton Flusk Index Market Lab is, what it is not, and the risks users should understand before reading market content.

What this site provides

  • Educational explanations of index-market concepts.
  • Plain-language market education.
  • Paper-trading risk boundaries.
  • Future brief, learning, glossary, and journal direction.

What this site does not provide

  • No live trade execution.
  • No brokerage account connection.
  • No guaranteed returns.
  • No personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Risk disclosure

Trading securities, ETFs, futures, options, or other financial instruments involves risk, including the risk of losing money. Futures and leveraged products can be especially risky and may not be suitable for beginners.

Paper trading can help a user practice decision-making, but paper results do not guarantee live trading results. Real trading can involve slippage, fees, liquidity constraints, execution delays, emotional pressure, and losses.

Users are responsible for their own decisions and should consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions.

Market data and commentary

Market commentary may be manually summarized, delayed, educational, or based on publicly discussed events. Do not treat a market brief as a real-time data feed or trading instruction.

Regulatory review

This page is not legal advice. If Milton Flusk later charges for trading content, gives security-specific recommendations, personalizes advice, or markets performance, the product should be reviewed by a qualified compliance or legal professional.
