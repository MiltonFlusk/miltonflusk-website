What this site does not provide

No trading service

  • No live trade execution.
  • No brokerage account connection.
  • No guaranteed returns.
  • No personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Risk disclosure

Trading securities, ETFs, futures, options, or other financial instruments involves risk, including the risk of losing money. Futures and leveraged products can be especially risky and may not be suitable for beginners.

Paper trading can help users practise decision-making, but paper results do not guarantee live trading results. Real trading can involve slippage, fees, liquidity constraints, execution delays, emotional pressure, and losses.

Users remain responsible for their own decisions and should consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions.

Market data and commentary

Any market commentary may be educational, delayed, manually summarized, or based on public context. Do not treat it as a real-time data feed or trading instruction.

Regulatory review

This page is not legal advice. If Milton Flusk later charges for market content, personalizes advice, or markets performance, the product should be reviewed by a qualified compliance or legal professional.
